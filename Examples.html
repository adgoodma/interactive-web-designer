Start with the feeling. Add a trigger.

Install the skill in a compatible host, then mention a capability and its trigger phrase in your request. Add your audience, content, colour palette and tone. The skill normally asks you to confirm your direction before building.

What counts as a trigger?

Chips below reproduce phrases from the skill's capability menu. They are natural-language cues, not slash commands or guarantees: actual skill discovery depends on the host. Examples are suggested prompts, not exact trigger definitions.

Exact menu phrases

Drag and drop & playable elements

Suggested wording, not a dedicated trigger

“Drag and drop” is not listed as a dedicated trigger phrase. Describe the behaviour you need and pair it with a relevant core capability.

Design an interactive website with a draggable card that people can drag and drop into a target area. Include a keyboard-accessible alternative.
